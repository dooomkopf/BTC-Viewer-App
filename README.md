Pre-built desktop binaries for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Download from [Releases](https://github.com/dooomkopf/BTC-Viewer-App/releases).

This is a Bitcoin price viewer focused on power-law charts and basic fits, which commercial viewers do not provide (logarithmic scaling of the time axis).

The fits are dynamically adjusted using sophisticated knowledge acquired over years of analyzing Bitcoin.

To reveal power-law growth we need a logarithmic scale on both axes and some quantile regressions:
- PL Bottom - the fundamentally most important bottom price due to network growth and economic mining activities
- PL Mean - the long-term average price
- Top Decay - the theoretical maximum values, which are likely determined by an exponential decay on top of the power law
